About us

Taraf Albahaa Company was established in 2025 to provide professional, high-quality and reliable services in cleaning, construction, maintenance and supporting services. The company is led by a young team and backed by well-skilled workers.

We approach every project with professionalism, dedication and attention to detail, and we measure our success by the satisfaction of our clients. We aim to build long-term partnerships as a trusted service provider in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Our vision. To be the leading industrial service provider, delivering world-class services that our clients prefer, with the best professional value on projects of any size.

Our mission. To meet our customers’ expectations at every level, with integrity and in line with our quality and ethics policies. We keep improving through up-to-date technology, staff development, and care for our employees and the environment.

What we do

Cleaning works

Professional cleaning for offices, facilities and industrial sites, with trained cleaning workers of all types.

Civil works

Infrastructure, roads and foundations, commercial and residential buildings, pre-engineered warehouses and factories, piling, and land development.

Mechanical works

Mechanical erection and installation, shutdowns, scaffolding, insulation, welding and fabrication, fire protection systems and HVAC for industrial plants.

Electrical works

Cable laying and splicing, generators, switchgear and transformers, transmission lines and substations, lighting and power installations, and SCADA systems.

Instrumentation works

Loop checks, fire and gas detection, control room equipment, field instrument installation and calibration, tubing work and fiber optic termination.

Piping works

Supply, fabrication and installation of stainless steel pipes and valves, hydrotesting, steam tracing, blasting and coating.

Plant maintenance

Tank repair, cleaning and painting, pipeline maintenance, shutdowns, and day-to-day upkeep of industrial plants.

Manpower supply

Engineers, welders, riggers, scaffolders, safety officers, inspectors, drivers, equipment operators, document controllers and office staff.

Catering services

Catering for offices, project sites and workforce accommodation, prepared and delivered to your schedule.

Travel logistics

Visa assistance and flight bookings, airport pickup and drop-off, hotel reservations, and local transportation.

Equipment and support

Heavy equipment rental for industrial and construction projects, and supporting services.
